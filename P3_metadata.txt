TITLE:
In-Context Binding Capacity in Language Models: What the Query and Readout Change

AUTHORS:
Manas Venkata Sai Ravulapalli; Samrath Chadha

AFFILIATION:
Efficient Computation Inc.

FIGURES: 5

ABSTRACT:
A low retrieval score can reflect the question and the rule used to select an answer, as well as a model's ability to associate names with values. We measure recall as the number of associations increases in 16 open models from 70M to 2.8B parameters, then compare questions using the same assignments. At sixteen bindings, deleting is from the original question raises Pythia-410M recall from 0.226 to 0.896 without changing the weights or scoring rule. In a preregistered comparison with the original question fixed, supervised hidden-state probes outperform trained candidate-logit decoders in all three held-out models. Two of those models also meet the registered ten-point probe advantage over native output. These results support testing the question and readout before treating poor recall as evidence that a larger model is needed.
